What these numbers are

They are totals for the whole chain, kept by the node in its value-pool index: for each block, how much YEC sits in outputs of each kind after that block. Every node running the same release reports the same figures for the same block. This page reads them from one node and adds nothing of its own.

Sapling and Sprout are the two shielded pools. Transparent (ECC) is YEC at ordinary transparent addresses, which are spent with an elliptic-curve signature. Post-quantum is YEC at addresses spent with a post-quantum signature, by scheme: Falcon (FN-DSA-512) or SLH-DSA (SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s). Vault is YEC locked in vaults. The rows add up to the total supply.

Monitored is the node's word for "this figure is available". A node that is still building its index, or cannot build it, reports a pool as not monitored; the page then shows a dash, never a guess.

Not shown: nothing here is about any address, transaction or person. The figures are sums over the whole chain and cannot be broken down further on this page.

Figures as JSON: current, series, per-block changes. 1 YEC = 100,000,000 zatoshis.
